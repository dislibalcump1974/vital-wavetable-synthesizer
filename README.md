# 💎 Vital  
**Wavetable Synthesizer by Matt Tytel**

**Vital** is a cutting-edge free wavetable synthesizer that combines visual sound design with high-quality synthesis.  
Developed by **Matt Tytel**, it features a fully animated interface that lets you literally see how your sound evolves.

## ✨ Features
- **Advanced Wavetable Engine** – morph between wavetables with spectral warping  
- **3 High-Quality Oscillators** – plus sample and noise oscillators  
- **Extensive Modulation** – drag-and-drop modulation with visual feedback  
- **Integrated FX Suite** – reverb, delay, distortion, compression, and chorus  
- **Visualized Filters & Envelopes** – real-time waveform animation  
- **Formats:** VST3 / AU / LV2 (Windows, macOS, Linux)

## 💻 Requirements
- Windows 10+ / macOS 10.13+ / Linux  
- 64-bit DAW supporting VST3 or AU  
- 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
-

Vital delivers the power of a premium synth — completely free.  
Its visual design helps beginners understand synthesis intuitively, while experts appreciate its precision and creative modulation depth.
